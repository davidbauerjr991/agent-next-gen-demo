# Event renders — P-LAYOUT-F3 15 (2026-11-01, at `99c3503`)

The 2026-11-01 baseline's cause 2: a card change re-renders most of the page. Measured with `node scripts/profile-renders.mjs <base url> <label> 5 events` on the dev server, under Phase 2. The script records every commit for 5 s after each of two events, with no settling first:

- **An arrival:** Maya Chen (hotkey B), answered on arrival.
- **An accept:** Tom Lewis (hotkey C) rings, then Accept. Recording starts at the hotkey, so this row is the ring and the accept together, and runs 5 s past the Accept click.

A commit's renders are credited to each of its origins, so the per-origin totals the script prints overlap; the numbers below are per commit.

## Before

| Event | Commits | Component renders | Largest commit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | 29 | 29,742 | 5,047 |
| Ring + accept | 34 | 27,223 | 2,301 |

Every large commit starts in `AgentWorkspaceAdvancedPage`'s own state, which is the workspace controller's state: the store's interactions, `useNavCards`' overlay, the live messages and utterances. It then reaches every region through `useWorkspace()`, a single context whose value is a new object on every render.

## What fans out

A probe during the arrival (13 controller renders, worktree only) counted the fields of the controller's result that got a new identity on each render. About 45 changed on every render, whether or not anything about them had changed. Among them:

- `interactions`, `scenarioCases`, `customerTable`, `homeTabs`, `tabApps`, `pinnedKeys`
- `navRailItems`, `leftNavAppItems`, `homeNavItem`, `restNavItems`, `appsMenuItems`, `appMenuGroups`
- `contentByPanelKey`, `searchContent`, `notifContent`, `scheduleContent`, `screenPopContent`, `contactDetailsTabContext`, `sessionDetailsTabContent`
- `contactCommands`, `incomingOffer`, `outboundDial`, `outboundConfig`, `threadMenu`, `omni`, `mainPins`
- `assignmentToasts`, `newOutboundLauncher`, `PANEL_KEY_METADATA`, `PANEL_KEY_INITIAL_ORDER`

Most of them hold functions or React elements, rebuilt by the controller's hooks on every render.

## Tried, not kept

Each was measured against the same two events, in a worktree only; neither change is in the repo.

| Change | Arrival renders | Ring + accept renders |
| --- | --- | --- |
| None (before) | 29,742 | 27,223 |
| A tracked workspace store: readers re-render only for the fields they read, with stable function fields, plus the nine page regions memoised | 27,667 | 24,844 |
| The same, plus structural sharing of equal plain data | 27,398 | 24,672 |

About 7–8% fewer renders, because the regions' fields are among the ~45 that change every render. A reader-tracking proxy over 78 call sites carries a real risk of stale renders, and that gain didn't justify it, so neither was committed.

## What would fix it

- Stable outputs from the controller: memoise its derived lists and objects (`useMemo` with real dependencies), and make its handlers stable (an event-handler ref pattern).
- Or split the result into narrower contexts by concern (cards, panels, header apps, outbound), which the context's own comment already planned for P2.0.

Either is a refactor of the controller (`useWsCards` … `useWsHeaderApps`, about 5,600 lines), not a selector change. It's recorded in PROGRESS Known gaps. The queue-tile tick stays for P-TIMERS, as planned.
